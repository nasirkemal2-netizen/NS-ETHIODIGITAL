# STEP4_SALES_PLAN.md
Sales plan for NS-ETHIODIGITAL

## Overview
This file describes the sales strategy, pricing, and distribution channels for the product.

## Sales Channels
- Direct sales to schools via representatives
- Online subscriptions via website or app
- Partner distribution: NGOs, educational networks

## Pricing Strategy
- Tiered pricing based on school size or number of users
- Optional trial or pilot programs for adoption
- Discounts for bulk or early subscriptions

## Sales Process
1. Identify target schools and decision-makers
2. Demonstrate product features and benefits
3. Provide pilot access and collect feedback
4. Convert to paid subscription
