# STEP3_MARKETING.md
Marketing strategy for NS-ETHIODIGITAL

## Overview
This file outlines the marketing approach, target audience, and positioning strategy for the product.

## Target Audience
- Schools (administrators, teachers)
- Students
- Education authorities and decision-makers

## Marketing Channels
- Online: social media, website, email campaigns
- Offline: seminars, workshops, school visits
- Partner channels: NGOs, educational networks

## Messaging & Positioning
- Emphasize ease-of-use, multi-school support, role-based access
- Highlight exam management, auto-grading, and result analytics
- Showcase pilot school feedback and success stories
