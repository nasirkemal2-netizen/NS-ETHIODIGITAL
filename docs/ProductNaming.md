# ProductNaming.md
Umbrella brand and product naming strategy for NS-ETHIODIGITAL

## Umbrella Brand
- NS-ETHIODIGITAL serves as the primary brand for all related educational digital services

## Product Naming Principles
- Consistency: All product names should align with the umbrella brand
- Clarity: Names should reflect the purpose/function
- Simplicity: Easy to remember and pronounce
- Scalability: Allow future products to fit under the same brand

## Example Product Names
- NS-EDU-EXAM: Online exam management module
- NS-EDU-ITEMBANK: Central question repository
- NS-EDU-ANALYTICS: Results and reporting dashboard
